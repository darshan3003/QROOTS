KBT030

Gulmohar Tree

Delonix regia

Gulmohar is a medium-sized, fast-growing ornamental tree famous for its wide, umbrella-shaped canopy and brilliant red-orange flowers. It is commonly planted along roads, in gardens, parks and educational institutions. Its spreading branches provide shade, while its colourful flowers make the tree especially attractive during the flowering season. It is also known as the Flame Tree or Royal Poinciana.
